## 2. Hard rules (never break these)

1. **The script is sacred.** Use it exactly as written in section 4. Do not add, remove, reword or reorder any spoken word.
2. **60 seconds maximum.** If it runs long, tighten only the explanation section (0:25 to 0:45) slightly. Never remove a marked pause.
3. **Pronunciation.**
   - "Spitch" is said **SPITCH** (IPA /spɪtʃ/), rhyming with "pitch". It must never sound like "speech".
   - "Wahb" is one syllable: **WAHB**.
   - "uOttawa" is said "you-Ottawa".
   - "résumé" is said the **French way**: ray-zoo-MAY (IPA /ʁe.zy.ˈme/), with a light French accent on that word only.
4. **No real brand marks.** No Tinder logo or Tinder-specific UI, no real photo or likeness of a Canadian bank note, no official uOttawa crest, no ChatGPT or OpenAI logo. Use stylized or illustrated versions.
5. **Captions** for every spoken line, burned in (see section 8).
6. **No invented claims.** The only statistic is "48,000 students (uOttawa, 2023)", taken from the brief. Verify it against uOttawa's published figures before publishing.

## 4. The script (verbatim)

Spoken exactly as below, in this order. `[PAUSE x s]` marks silence (faint music allowed unless stated).

```
Did you ever just have the idea?  [PAUSE 1 s]
The path to financial freedom,
then it died in an AI chat box.  [PAUSE 1 s]
because you didn't have the means to push it through and see it live?
Or is the projects section on your resume looking a little... rough?
And you're tired of all those 'thanks for your application' emails?
Introducing...  [PAUSE 1 s]  Spitch.
It's a way to broadcast your idea, get visibility, partnerships and collaborations.
Pitch it in less than a minute, post it, and people swipe right or left, based on what they see, to connect with you and push it forward.
Pitchers and builders, those with the means and those with the skills, connecting for the price of a coffee.
The market starts here, at uOttawa.
I'm a fourth-year engineering student. I live this problem.  [PAUSE 1 s]
If you think this would make a great LinkedIn post, let's chat.  [PAUSE 0.5 s]
Or swipe right.
```

Script note: the delivery notes once say "I lived this problem", but the script says "I live this problem". The script wins: "I live".
